Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 • Compliant with Google Play & Indus Appstore Developer Policies.

1. Overview & Core Philosophy
Docunova ("we", "our", or "app") is designed with an offline-first, privacy-by-default architecture. We believe your sensitive documents, financial receipts, contracts, and personal IDs belong exclusively to you. Docunova does not harvest, sell, monetize, or transmit your document contents to third-party advertisers.
2. Google Drive Cloud Integration
When you voluntarily connect your Google account, Docunova requests only the restricted https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This restricted permission strictly limits the app to viewing, creating, and modifying only files created by Docunova itself inside your dedicated "DocuNova" folder. Docunova cannot read, view, modify, or delete any of your other private Google Drive files or photos.
3. Android Device Permissions Explained
  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required strictly for real-time document capture and edge auto-detection in the scanner viewfinder. Camera frames are processed in-memory and are never sent to external servers.
  • Storage / Media Access: Needed to save your generated high-resolution PDF and Word (.docx) files to your device's local Documents and Download folders.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Used solely when you choose to sync files to your Google Drive or use cloud translation APIs. All network traffic is encrypted via TLS 1.3/HTTPS.
4. On-Device OCR & Translation Processing
Text extraction is performed on-device using Google ML Kit. Your documents remain local to your hardware unless you actively tap the "Sync to Drive" or "Translate" button. No biometric, face, or profile profiling data is gathered or stored.
5. Data Deletion & Developer Contact
Because files are stored locally on your device or in your personal Google Drive, you retain full control to delete them at any time. To request data inquiries or assistance, contact our developer support team directly at:

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Everything you need to know about Docunova's features and compatibility.

Does Docunova really export 100% watermark-free documents?
Yes! Unlike many other scanner apps that slap huge "Scanned by..." logos on your pages unless you pay, Docunova produces completely clean, professional, watermark-free PDFs and Word documents.
Can I use Docunova completely offline without an internet connection?
Absolutely. You can tap "Use Offline without Sign-In" directly on the login screen. Camera scanning, edge detection, image filters, on-device OCR, and PDF/DOCX export work without any network connection.
How does Docunova convert scans into editable Word (.docx) files?
Docunova extracts text elements using Google ML Kit neural vision, reconstructs paragraphs with layout preservation, and encodes them natively into real Microsoft Word OpenXML (.docx) documents using Apache POI.
Which Android versions and architectures are supported?
Docunova is optimized for Android 9.0 (API Level 28) up through Android 15 (API Level 35) on modern 64-bit and 32-bit ARM architectures (arm64-v8a & armeabi-v7a).
